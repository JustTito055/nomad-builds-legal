Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Nomad Builds Automation only accesses the data required to post content to the Nomad Builds channel's own connected accounts via each platform's official API.

  • From TikTok we receive the connected account's basic profile information (such as display name and avatar) and the permission to upload and publish videos.
  • No personal data from viewers or followers is collected, stored, or shared.
  • Access tokens are used only to publish content to the Nomad Builds accounts and are stored securely by the channel owner.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services. Contact us to have any stored data deleted.
