Nomad Builds

Nomad Builds is a content channel that publishes short-form videos on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. Behind the channel runs Nomad Builds Automation, our own publishing tool that takes a finished, approved video and posts it to the channel's accounts.

How the publishing tool works

  1. Connect TikTok. The channel owner signs in with TikTok through TikTok's official Login Kit and approves the requested permissions. Nomad Builds Automation never sees or stores the TikTok password.
  2. Prepare the video. A finished video is placed in the publishing queue together with its caption.
  3. Review and approve. The channel owner checks the video, caption and privacy setting before anything is posted.
  4. Publish. The tool uploads the approved video to the connected TikTok account through the official Content Posting API and checks that the post went through.

What we use from TikTok

  • user.info.basic and user.info.profile: to show which TikTok account is connected, so the owner can confirm videos go to the right account.
  • video.upload and video.publish: to upload and post the approved videos to that account.

We only use this access to publish Nomad Builds content. See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
